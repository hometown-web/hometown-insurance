No one is ever ready when that fender bender happens. Giving you an idea of what to expect is often how we can best help you as your agent. We can provide you with guidance about the claims process.

What about when it is only your car involved with a curb or tree? “Should I file a claim?” is the question we hear most often in this case. That decision is up to you. Cost is a factor, and does the amount meet your deductible? A terrific new tool helps Hometown provide you with a preliminary estimate for repairs and make that decision a little easier.

The tool is an app you can use on your phone or desktop which allows you to upload photos of the physical damage to your vehicle from different angles. These photos are then analyzed and an estimated cost for repair is given. Using this information you can decide if it meets your deductible and if the amount is worth a claim.
