May is Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month! Hometown Insurance offers motorcycle and other vehicle coverage and brings you these Quick Tips for safe riding from the Motorcycle Safety Foundation.

<strong>QUICK TIPS: General Guidelines for Riding a Motorcycle Safely </strong>

<strong>Be visible: </strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Remember that motorists often have trouble seeing motorcycles and reacting in time.</li>
 	<li>Make sure your headlight works and is on day and night.</li>
 	<li>Use reflective strips or decals on your clothing and on your motorcycle.</li>
 	<li>Be aware of the blind spots cars and trucks have.</li>
 	<li>Flash your brake light when you are slowing down and before stopping.</li>
 	<li>If a motorist doesn’t see you, don’t be afraid to use your horn.</li>
</ul>
<strong>Dress for safety: </strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Wear a quality helmet and eye protection.</li>
 	<li>Wear bright clothing and a light-colored helmet.</li>
 	<li>Wear leather or other thick, protective clothing.</li>
 	<li>Choose long sleeves and pants, over-the-ankle boots, and gloves.</li>
 	<li>Remember – the only thing between you and the road is your protective gear.</li>
</ul>
<strong>Apply effective mental strategies: </strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Constantly search the road for changing conditions. Use MSF’s Search, Evaluate, Execute strategy (<strong>SEE</strong><sup>SM</sup>) to increase time and space safety margins.</li>
 	<li>Give yourself space and time to respond to other motorists’ actions.</li>
 	<li>Give other motorists time and space to respond to you.</li>
 	<li>Use lane positioning to be seen; ride in the part of a lane where you are most visible.</li>
 	<li>Watch for turning vehicles.</li>
 	<li>Signal your next move in advance.</li>
 	<li>Avoid weaving between lanes.</li>
 	<li>Pretend you’re invisible, and ride extra defensively.</li>
 	<li>Don't ride when you are tired or under the influence of alcohol or other drugs.</li>
 	<li>Know and follow the rules of the road, and stick to the speed limit.</li>
</ul>
<strong>Know your bike and how to use it: </strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Get formal training and take refresher courses.</li>
 	<li>Call 800.446.9227 or visit www.msf-usa.org to locate the Motorcycle Safety Foundation hands-on <em>RiderCourse</em><sup>SM </sup>nearest you.</li>
 	<li>Practice. Develop your riding techniques before going into heavy traffic. Know how to handle your bike in conditions such as wet or sandy roads, high winds, and uneven surfaces.</li>
</ul>
<strong><em>Remember: Give yourself space. People driving cars often just don’t see motorcycles. Even when drivers do see you, chances are they’ve never been on a motorcycle and can’t properly judge your speed.</em></strong>
