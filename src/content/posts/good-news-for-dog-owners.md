<p>Great news for dog owners! A law taking effect in March adds new restrictions to homeowners insurers’ ability to underwrite breeds of dogs residing in a home or apartment. The law, which builds on one that took effect a year ago, effectively requires insurers to provide liability coverage at full policy limits for most dogs regardless of breed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The New York State Assembly approved the bill last March by a vote of 115 to 32, and the Senate followed in May by a vote of 49 to 13. Gov. Kathy Hochul signed it into law on December 15, 2022.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Previously, insurers were prohibited from:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Refusing to issue or renew</p>
<p>• Cancelling, or</p>
<p>• Surcharging a rate or premium</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Based solely on the breed of a dog in the household.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The amended law, which applies to policies issued, renewed, modified, altered or amended on and after March 15, 2023, further prohibits insurers from:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Excluding</p>
<p>• Limiting</p>
<p>• Restricting, or</p>
<p>• Reducing coverage</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Based on dog breed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The law applies to homeowners and renters insurance policies. It also applies to commercial policies insuring one- to four-family dwellings if the named insured is an actual person and not an organization such as a limited liability company or a corporation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Be aware that after March 14, your carriers will no longer be able to add exclusions or sub-limits to the liability coverage in any of these types of policies based solely on dog breeds.</p>
