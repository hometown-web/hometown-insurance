<p>Put Christmas tree safety on top of your list!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To help prevent Christmas tree fires, take the pledge to follow these simple instructions:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<ul class="customIndent">
<p>• When you purchase a live tree, make sure it is fresh. Needles should be green and springy to the touch.</p>
<p>• Cut 1 to 2 inches off the bottom of the trunk before placing the tree in the stand. A fresh cut allows the tree to absorb water. Place the tree in water right away.</p>
<p>• Fill the tree stand with water every day.</p>
<p>• Make sure the tree is at least 3 feet away from any heat source, like floor vents, fireplaces, radiator, candles or lamps.</p>
<p>• Make sure your tree is not blocking an exit.</p>
<p>• Always turn the tree lights off or unplug them before going to bed or leaving your home.</p>
<p>• Use lights that are approved by a national testing organization like UL.</p>
<p>• Replace lights that have worn or broken cords or loose bulb connections.</p>
<p>• Follow manufacturer's instructions for the number of light strands you can safely connect. Make sure you are using the right lights for indoors or outdoors.</p>
<p>• Remove your tree immediately after the holidays even if you think it is not dry. </p>
</ul>
