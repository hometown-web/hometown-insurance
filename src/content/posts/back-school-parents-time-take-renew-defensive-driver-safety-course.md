<strong>When you complete a New York State approved defensive driving class</strong>
<ul>
	<li>your driving knowledge will be refreshed</li>
	<li>you can reduce up to 4 points from your NYS DMV driving record (learn about the <a href="http://dmv.ny.gov/node/1386">NYS driver point system</a>)</li>
	<li>you will receive a 10% reduction of the base rate of your auto and motorcycle insurance premiums each year for three years</li>
</ul>
<strong>A convenient option is to take the on-line course </strong>
<ul>
	<li>100% Online</li>
	<li>You can come and go on your schedule</li>
</ul>
&nbsp;

<strong>To sign up for the reduced cost on-line defensive driver safety course go to:</strong>
<ul>
	<li><a href="http://www.empiresafetycouncil.com/hometown%20">www.empiresafetycouncil.com/hometown </a></li>
	<li>Enter the promo code: <strong>HOMETOWN</strong> for a $15 discount, your total cost for the on-line course will then be <strong>$29.95</strong>.</li>
	<li>Please note the on-line course has time limits and requirements, please follow the instructions carefully</li>
</ul>
<strong>Once you complete your Defensive Driver Safety course</strong>
<ul>
	<li>Your Certificate of Completion is mailed directly to you</li>
	<li>Make a copy of your certificate and send to your Hometown Personal Account Representative so we can provide to your Insurance company</li>
	<li>You must provide the certificate <strong>within 90 days</strong> after course completion</li>
	<li>The reduction will be retroactive to the date you completed the course</li>
</ul>
