<img class="alignleft size-full wp-image-3354" alt="fdfdfdfd" src="https://assets.hometowninsurance.com/media/fdfdfdfd.png" width="100" />The purpose of the Association is to reduce firefighter and EMS death and injury for all incidents.  They will accomplish this by providing all members a place to meet and discuss issues. The Association will provide education and training to all who belong and will be the fastest, least expensive and easiest solution to improve incident safety.

There are no dues, just free and informative information.  Please visit their website at www.btsoa.org or call Jeff Bailes at 631-736-5432 for more information.
