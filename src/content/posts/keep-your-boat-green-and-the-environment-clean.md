<p class="has-text-align-center"><strong>Clean Boating Tips:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1 - Clean safely.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Look for the EPA-Certified “Design for the Environment” DfE label, to identify cleaning products to help protect the environment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>2 - Chart your course.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Study your waterways to prevent boat propellers from damaging sensitive habitats or injuring marine life.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>3 - Recycle your waste.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dispose of paints, batteries, antifreeze, cleaning products, oil and other hazardous wastes at a waste collection facility when you return to land.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>4 - Stow your trash.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Keep trash onboard and never throw plastic bags, soda cans, cigarette butts, fishing line, or any garbage into the water.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>5 - Recycle your monofilament fishing line.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Protect marine life by properly disposing of monofilament fishing line at nearby marine accessories stores and shore-side recycling locations.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
