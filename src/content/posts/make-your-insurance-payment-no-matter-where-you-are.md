Online portals, available from the insurance companies, make it easier to manage your bills. They can be a good option to keep timely payments. Insurance companies are becoming more strict with deadlines for payment. An even better option is Automatic Payment Plans: so easy, no need to go out in the cold!

In our busy world, automatic payments or recurring credit card payments can help you make your payments on time and save on installment fees.

Save time and money. Automatically.
<ul>
	<li>No checks</li>
	<li>No stamps</li>
	<li>Reduce or eliminate Installment Fees</li>
</ul>
Choose one of two ways to pay your policy bill automatically:
<ul>
	<li>Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT): Pay through your checking or savings account*</li>
	<li>Recurring Credit Card (RCC): Pay through your VISA® or MasterCard®*</li>
</ul>
Either way, your bill will be paid whether you’re on vacation, stuck in the snow or just too busy to spend time paying bills.
<ul>
	<li>No missed payments</li>
	<li>No worries</li>
</ul>
*Service charges may apply to Recurring Credit Card and Electronic Funds Transfer payments.
