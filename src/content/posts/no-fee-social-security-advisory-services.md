AMAC Foundation is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization dedicated to serving seniors in a variety of ways, including provision of credible and reliable guidance intended to improve their understanding of areas affecting older Americans. The AMAC Foundation helps individuals navigate senior needs, especially in relation to Social Security education. Representatives at the Foundation have earned certification as National Social Security Advisors, qualifying them to counsel retirees and pre-retirees on questions and issues pertaining to Social Security. The training preceding the qualification exam equips these Advisors to provide proper guidance to clients on the many Social Security options available and enables them to provide a trusted service to the public. Certification is accredited through the Ohio-based National Social Security Association (NSSA).

Our professionals are available to handle questions submitted to the Foundation. To submit a question or to request information:

<strong>By Telephone:</strong> Call 888-750-2622
<strong>Via Email:</strong> Send to <a href="mailto:SSAdvisor@AmacFoundation.org">SSAdvisor@AmacFoundation.org</a>
<strong>In Person Florida:</strong> Call for appointment (888-750-2622) at the AMAC Foundation office (312 Teague Trail, Lady Lake, Florida 32159)

“The certification of our staff members is critical to our mission of supporting and educating America’s seniors,” commented AMAC Foundation Former President Dan Weber, “and we believe that providing credible, unbiased information to people in, or aging into, Social Security is an important part of what the Foundation does. And, since the Foundation is a tax-exempt organization relying solely on contributions from the public to operate, there is no charge for this valuable service.”
