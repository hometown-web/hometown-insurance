With the influx of the destruction and damage inflicted by the recent Hurricanes Harvey and Irma, what if a catastrophic hurricane or severe snowstorm should hit our area?  Are you ready or would your businesses be severely damaged or destroyed?

We have provided a Business Continuity Planning Guide that clearly identifies 7 planning steps necessary to developing a strategy for ensuring that your personnel and assets are protected and able to function in the event of a disaster.

A lifetime spent building a company can be destroyed in days by a natural disaster. The guide includes key steps that must be implemented, including:
<ul>
 	<li>Organizing Recovery Teams</li>
 	<li>Developing Relocation Plans</li>
 	<li>Writing Business Continuity Procedures</li>
 	<li>Documenting Manual Workarounds</li>
 	<li>Developing an IT Recovery Strategy</li>
</ul>
