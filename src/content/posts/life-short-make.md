Do you like to travel to exotic destinations? Hiking or rock climbing? Enjoy water skiing? Does your family share your love of adventure and are they part of your plan?

Travel, boating, water and outdoor sports, summer camps, and parties are popular summer activities. As you plan any of these activities, consider calling us, your independent insurance agent, to ask about specific coverage for these risks. Summer should be a fun carefree time and insurance can help keep it that way.

Your independent agent can shop for you to find the right coverage for your boat or watercraft and the liability or property protection you may need. We can review your homeowners or renters insurance policy to check that your liability limits safeguard your assets and that you have the correct endorsements for replacement of lost or stolen jewelry and special items.

If you have a pool, family dog, plan any parties, have children or a trampoline, it may also be the right time to buy an umbrella policy! An umbrella policy provides an extra level of legal and liability coverage for peace of mind.

Auto policies today can include optional features that help when you travel such as: trip interruption, theft of property from the vehicle and towing. Call us and ask to review your policy for premier roadside assistance and help keep your travel peaceful.

Fun should be just that…fun! Prepare ahead of activities by conducting an insurance check-up with us so your adventure includes only the pleasant surprises!
