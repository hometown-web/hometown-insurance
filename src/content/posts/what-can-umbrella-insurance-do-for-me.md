<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you own a home, car or boat, you’re likely to have primary insurance policies that provide liability coverage for each of these assets. However, your primary policy limits may not be enough to cover all claimed damages, which can cause unexpected financial hardship. That’s where umbrella insurance comes in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Umbrella liability insurance is designed to further protect against the costs of:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Claims made against you for bodily injury to others due to a car accident, boat incident, fall or other covered accidents.</p>
<p>• Claims made against you for covered damage to others’ property.</p>
<p>• Personal liability for claims like libel, slander, defamation of character and invasion of privacy.</p>
<p>• Potential out-of-pocket expenses related to each of these (or other) covered incidents. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Additionally, with increasing dollar amounts awarded by juries in personal auto liability cases – driven partially by social inflation1 – it’s essential to safeguard your income and assets.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Who should get umbrella insurance?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Anyone who has assets that they want to protect should consider purchasing umbrella insurance. Here are some examples to think about:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• <strong>At-home dangers</strong> – <a href="https://www.travelers.com/resources/home/safety/yard-safety" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Trampolines</a> and <a href="https://www.travelers.com/resources/home/safety/swimming-pool-safety-tips" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">swimming pools</a> could potentially lead to serious injuries and liability claims.</p>
<p>• <strong>Slip and fall claims</strong> – Someone could slip and fall – and suffer an injury – on your property. This can apply to your primary residence or if you’re a landlord of one or more additional properties.  </p>
<p>• <strong>Family matters </strong>– Imagine your liability if your <a href="https://www.travelers.com/resources/auto/teen-driving/12-tips-to-help-teens-drive-safely" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">teenager</a> causes a major car accident.  </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These are just a few examples of the risks that might make umbrella insurance right for you. Our Hometown Insurance agents can outline additional potential risks you may face.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Another important reason to consider purchasing umbrella insurance: peace of mind. In life, mistakes and accidents happen. Someone, including friends or visiting loved ones, could become seriously injured on your property or when you’re driving.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When something like this occurs, you’ll know that you took extra steps to help cover the damages in the event you’re responsible for the loss.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How much umbrella coverage do I need?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Umbrella policies range in limits, typically starting at $1 million and going up to $10 million. To help determine how much coverage you should consider, start by totaling up the value of your property and financial assets, such as:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Cars, boats, other vehicles.</p>
<p>• Jewelry, art, antiques, and other valuables.</p>
<p>• Home equity or equity in other properties you own.</p>
<p>• Cash, savings accounts, CDs, bonds.</p>
<p>• 401(k), IRA, other retirement accounts.</p>
<p>• Stocks, bonds, and other investment accounts.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Keep in mind that the specific assets you could lose as the result of a judgment may vary by state. Once you’ve calculated the total value of your assets, ask your Hometown Insurance representative for guidance on the amount of umbrella coverage that makes sense for you.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It’s also a good idea to review your coverage periodically and make adjustments as needed to reflect any changes to your assets over time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How much does umbrella insurance cost?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Typically, umbrella coverage is considered very affordable. Your final cost will depend on variables specific to your situation. Talk to your Hometown Insurance representative about whether you can save money on your premiums by bundling policies with the same insurance carrier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Is an umbrella policy worth it?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you’re thinking about adding umbrella coverage, consider this: You hope you’ll never have to use it, but if you do, you’ll be grateful it’s there. Umbrella coverage is designed to take effect once the liability limits of your homeowners, auto, or boat policies have been exceeded. It’s an economical way to safeguard your assets now to help avoid the possibility of significant financial hardship later.</p>
