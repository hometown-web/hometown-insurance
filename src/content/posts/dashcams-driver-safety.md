<p>Dashcams can play a significant role in mitigating risks and enhancing driver safety as both a proactive and reactive tool. Installing dashcams into company vehicles leads to an approximate 60% reduction in collisions, according to a 2017 study published in the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022437516305138?via%3Dihub">Journal of Safety Research</a>. This technology can provide insight into driving behaviors, safety alerts, and coaching opportunities, as well as encourage driver accountability.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Insight into Driver Behaviors</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Insights gathered from dashcam technology can provide educational opportunities for companies and drivers alike.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Safety Alerts</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Some dashcam systems come with advanced safety features, such as collision detection and lane departure warnings, which can alert drivers of potential hazards. These alerts can reduce the risk of accidents and provide key takeaways and real-time suggestions to curtail unsafe driving habits, such as distracted or <a href="https://www.merchantsgroup.com/blog/drowsy-driving-is-dangerous/">drowsy</a> driving, which are among the top causes of accidents in the United States.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dashcams can often detect dangerous driving practices such as speeding, harsh braking, tailgating, or lane departure, and can trigger alerts, allowing drivers to correct these behaviors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Coaching Opportunities</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dashcam technology can provide companies with coaching opportunities to improve driving safety and efficiency. When a company installs dashcams in their fleet, managers can review driving habits, identifying top-performing drivers who consistently practice safe driving and drivers exhibiting more reckless behaviors. Managers can then recognize and reward safe drivers, creating positive reinforcement for these behaviors and encouraging others to practice safe driving, too!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Detailed footage that dashcams provide can help in numerous ways, from evaluating a fleet’s driving practice and identifying areas of improvement to optimizing routes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Driver Accountability</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Installing dashcams into personal or commercial vehicles can often encourage driver accountability. Drivers with dashcams in their vehicles tend to drive more responsibly and are often more cautious! With the knowledge that they are being recorded, drivers tend to follow traffic laws more strictly and avoid aggressive and unsafe driving behaviors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Other Benefits of Dashcam Installation</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In addition to encouraging and prioritizing driver safety, there are numerous other benefits to installing dashcams in commercial and personal vehicles, such as saving money and expediting claim resolution.
