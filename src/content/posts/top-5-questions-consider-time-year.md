Here are five questions that you should definitely consider asking at this time of year:

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

&nbsp;
<ol>
	<li>Is there an engagement or marriage in your near future or newly inherited jewelry?</li>
	<li>Will you be adding bicycles, recreational equipment or other outdoor higher cost items to your fun list?</li>
	<li>Are your electronics covered under extended plans?</li>
	<li>Have you checked your Home inside and out regarding: gutter condition, tree health, and soil grade?</li>
	<li>Does your existing homeowner or renter policy include enough replacement cost insurance to replace these items?</li>
</ol>
&nbsp;

Ask your Hometown Agency representative about a Personal Floater and other insurance protection to expand on the coverage offered on your existing homeowner or renter policy.
