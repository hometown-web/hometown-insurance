<strong>Did you know that how you choose to pay your insurance premium can save you money? </strong>

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

<strong>Your choices for paying insurance premiums include:</strong>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Payment/installment plans – insurance companies offer 3 month, 6 month, 9 month or 12 month plans that include service fees. </strong></li>
	<li><strong>Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT)</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Pay in Full</strong></li>
	<li><strong>Or in the case of homeowner’s insurance, mortgagee bill as part of your monthly escrow account. </strong></li>
</ul>
<strong> </strong>

<strong>The option to pay in installments can make a payment manageable and service fees can be inexpensive at 4, 6 and 10 dollars, they do still add up. You may be able to save money by signing up for Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) or paying the bill in full. </strong>

<strong> </strong>

<strong>Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) is an excellent way to have the premium paid on time and the funds are automatically deducted from your savings or checking account. </strong>

<strong>The benefits of EFT:</strong>
<ul>
	<li>Often you can choose the deduction date</li>
	<li>Save money on installment fees, with an average zero or $1 fee</li>
	<li>Transactions are secure</li>
	<li>Makes bill paying effortless</li>
	<li>No checks, stamp, missed payments</li>
	<li>Choose monthly or one lump sum</li>
	<li>Deductions continue for future renewals unless you cancel your authorization</li>
</ul>
Paid in full plans may offer a discount with some carriers ranging from 5%-30% on the total 6 month or annual premium.
