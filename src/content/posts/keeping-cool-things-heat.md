With hot weather just around the corner, spring or early summer is the ideal time to do a check-up on your HVAC cooling systems.   Have a qualified HVAC professional conduct a maintenance check-up on cooling systems which can:
<ul>
 	<li>Prevent small problems from becoming expensive repairs</li>
 	<li>Avoid long waits and high repair prices during peak season</li>
 	<li>Ensure system is working efficiently for air quality and to save on utility bills</li>
</ul>
Here are some steps the HVAC maintenance visit can include:

<strong>Air Conditioning Maintenance Checklist</strong>
<ul>
 	<li>Check refrigerant levels</li>
 	<li>Check electrical components / controls (use lockout tagout procedures)</li>
 	<li>Clean evaporator / condenser coils (follow PPE protocols)</li>
 	<li>Oil motor</li>
 	<li>Calibrate thermostat</li>
 	<li>Check condenser</li>
 	<li>Check, clean or replace filters (use properly MERV rated filters)</li>
</ul>
