<ul>
	<li>prep time 25 min</li>
	<li>total time 45 min</li>
	<li>ingredients 5</li>
	<li>servings 8</li>
</ul>
<b>Ingredients:</b>

1 box Pillsbury® refrigerated pie crusts, softened as directed on box

2 cups diced peeled apples

1/3 cup sugar

1 tablespoon all-purpose flour

1 ½ teaspoons ground cinnamon

<b>Steps</b>
<ol>
	<li> Heat oven to 400°F. Unroll pie crusts on work surface. Cut each crust into quarters, making 8 wedges.</li>
	<li> Mix apples, sugar, flour and cinnamon. Top half of each crust wedge with 1/3 cup apple mixture. Fold un-topped sides of wedges over filling. With fork, press edges to seal. Place on ungreased cookie sheet. Cut several small slits in top of each to allow steam to escape.</li>
	<li> Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until light golden brown. Serve warm or cool.</li>
</ol>
One can (21 oz) of apple pie filling can be used in place of the apples, sugar, flour and cinnamon.

For a special touch, brush water or egg white over tops of unbaked pockets and sprinkle lightly with cinnamon-sugar. Bake as directed.
