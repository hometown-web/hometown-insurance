<p>DATA BREACH PREVENTION</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>According to Visa, 85 percent of data breaches occur at small businesses. Further, 60 percent of small businesses victimized by cybercrime shut down within six months.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Any business that transmits, collects or stores private information, or works with those who do, is at risk for a data breach. Private information includes social security numbers, bank account numbers, credit card numbers, personnel files, medical records and more.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As per Jenn Barrett, Director of Commercial Lines Operations at Hometown Insurance Agency:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>“Virtual and remote work has been an eye opener for our small business clients, they are far more susceptible to the risk of cyber-attacks. The employee’s home firewalls do not frequently offer the same protection as their office’s secure system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Small businesses are the least prepared to deal with the financial impact or educate themselves about the issues that can arise.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Another coverage, social engineering may not be available on a cyber-policy but may be available on a crime policy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
