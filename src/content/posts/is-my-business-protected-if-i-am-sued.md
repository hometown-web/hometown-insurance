<p><strong>What is Professional Liability Insurance?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Professional liability insurance, also called errors and omissions insurance (E&amp;O insurance) or indemnity insurance, protects your business if you are sued for negligently performing your services, even if you have not made a mistake.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A customer could sue you because you did something you should not have, or you did not do something you should have, in the course of providing professional services. A professional liability insurance policy could protect you by covering the cost to defend yourself, as well as any settlement or judgment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Why do I need Professional Liability Insurance?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Professional liability insurance coverage gives your business protection from the risks you face in delivering your professional services. The section below discusses everything our professional liability insurance coverage offers since we want you to feel secure about what your policy does and does not cover. You should consider professional liability insurance coverage if your business:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Provides a professional service.</p>
<p>• Regularly gives advice to clients.</p>
<p>• Is requested by a client to have professional liability insurance to complete a contract.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>How much does Professional Liability Insurance cost?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The cost of professional liability insurance coverage varies based on the size of your business, the industry you are in, your location, and the amount of coverage you need.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>What does Professional Liability Insurance cover?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Alleged or Actual Negligence</p>
<p>• Defense Costs</p>
<p>• Personal Injury (e.g., Libel or Slander)</p>
<p>• Temporary Staff and Independent Contractors</p>
<p>• Claims Arising from Services Provided in the Past</p>
<p>• Worldwide Coverage</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>What your Professional Liability Insurance policy does not cover:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We want our customers to fully understand what they are buying. Below is a summary of some of the key exclusions of your professional liability policy. For complete details of all the exclusions, please read the policy wording.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Bodily Injury or Property Damage</p>
<p>• Fraudulent Acts</p>
<p>• Employment Matters</p>
<p>• False Advertising</p>
<p>• Personally Identifiable Information</p>
<p>• Other Services</p>

<p><strong>Get Liability Insurance that best meets your needs</strong></p>
