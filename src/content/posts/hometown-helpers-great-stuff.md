Our Hometown Insurance personnel are some terrific people! They work tirelessly to provide high quality insurance options for our customers and they generously donate to our community. For example, in August, the Hometown team collectively donated for back-to-school backpacks given to school children at Habitat for Humanity of Suffolk.<img class="alignright size-medium wp-image-4955" src="https://assets.hometowninsurance.com/media/ht-helpers-school-supplies-300x146.png" alt="ht-helpers-school-supplies" width="300" height="146" />
