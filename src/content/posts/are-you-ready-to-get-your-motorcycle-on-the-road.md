<p>Your motorcycle gets you out on the open road in a way that can feel like pure freedom. Before you hit the road, you will want to make sure you have the coverage you need, should the unexpected happen.</p>

<p>Let’s face it. It’s important to have motorcycle insurance. Nearly every state requires proof of motorcycle insurance before you can register your bike. Beyond the state requirements, having motorcycle insurance is important to help protect you, and your bike, in case of an accident.</p>

<p><strong>Qualifying Bikes and Vehicles</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are a variety of bikes that qualify for motorcycle coverage. These include motorcycles from manufacturers such as Harley-Davidson, Honda and Indian, as well as models ranging from cruisers to touring to sport bikes and custom bikes. Coverage is also available for two-wheeled rides like scooters and mopeds and off-road vehicles** such as dirt bikes, ATVs, UTVs and snowmobiles.</p>
