<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Backyard entertaining has gotten more elaborate in recent years. Fire pits are helping homeowners extend the backyard season, and today’s playsets involve more than a simple set of swings and a slide. Whether backyard entertaining means spending time by the swimming pool or gathered around the grill, here are some safety tips to help keep your oasis fun and free of danger.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Garden Water Features</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fish ponds, waterfalls and other water features can add a note of serenity to the backyard. They are also particularly attractive to young children, and can be a drowning hazard if proper protective steps are not put in place:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Children may drown in as little as an inch or two of water. An adult should watch children at all times when in or near water.</p>
<p>• Use a rigid, lockable cover or fence in all four sides as you would for a swimming pool.</p>
<p>• Tightly cover water treatment or chemical mixtures after use.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Trampolines</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Trampolines are fun, but according to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, they are the cause of nearly 100,000 injuries each year. Avoid being a statistic:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Limit the number of jumpers to one at a time.</p>
<p>• Supervision is needed for children at all times.</p>
<p>• Remove trampoline ladders after use to prevent unsupervised access by young children.</p>
<p>• Trampoline enclosures can help prevent injuries from falls from trampolines.</p>
<p>• Anchor the trampoline and enclosure to the ground by using a trampoline anchor kit.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Playsets</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Playsets can be enjoyed by children of all ages, but damaged or weather-worn playsets can cause accidents. Stay safe by:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Supervising children.</p>
<p>• Regularly checking for sturdiness, rusty bolts and wood rot, and making necessary repairs immediately.</p>
<p>• Inspecting playsets for openings between pieces that could trap a child’s head or neck.</p>
<p>• Placing playsets securely on level ground and on wood chips or other soft materials to cushion falls and help prevent injuries.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Fire Pits</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Warm nights are the perfect time to roast marshmallows. But inadequate supervision or improper use of fire pits can cause injury. Be smart:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>• Place the fire pit in a safe spot away from your home, backyard deck or low-hanging tree branches.</p>
<p>• Always require adult supervision around the fire pit while it is in use and until it has cooled off.</p>
<p>• Never leave the fire unattended.</p>
<p>• Use sand to fully extinguish the fire.</p>
<p>• Let the coals cool completely and dispose of them in a metal container.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Lawn Mowers</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lawn mowers can easily cause injury. Follow these tips for safety:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Read the mower’s manual, heed safety and operating instructions and learn the controls.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Do not allow children to ride as passengers on a riding lawn mower, and keep children a safe distance away, and preferably, out of the yard altogether while mowing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Clean up toys and other objects, such as rocks, from the yard to help prevent injuries to you, your loved ones, and pets due to flying objects.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Never leave a running mower unattended.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Never operate the mower in an enclosed place where carbon monoxide can accumulate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Never unclog or work on a lawn mower while the engine is on or when the spark plug is connected.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>• Wear non-slip shoes or boots (no open toes or heels), and hearing and eye protection. It is also preferable to wear long pants while mowing and/or trimming your lawn.</p>
